### How to play

1. Open the link above in **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge** on a computer.
2. Click anywhere to start. Sound and music begin with your first click.
3. Pick a mission: **01 Jupiter**, **02 Mars**, or **03 ISS**.

The first visit downloads about 100 MB of NASA models and imagery, so give it a moment on slow internet.

### Use your phone as the controller

1. In the game, choose **Phone controller**.
2. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera, or open the link shown and type the 4-digit PIN.
3. Both devices need internet. If they don't connect, put the phone and the computer on the same Wi-Fi or a mobile hotspot.

Keyboard and mouse also work, with no phone needed.
